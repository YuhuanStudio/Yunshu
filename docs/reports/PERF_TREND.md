### omni speech-IN → speech-out (the flagship round-trip)

_Measured 2026-06-29, M3 Max 36 GB, Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct-4bit, chunk_size=10,

| path | TTFT (s) | first_audio (s) | total (s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| text-in → speech (baseline) | 0.45 | 1.09 | ~2.7 |
